# Audience

## Purpose
Define who AST Studio is built for, what they need, and how the product fits into their creative lives.

## Primary User Personas
Describe the core user types who will rely on AST Studio.

## Secondary User Personas
List additional user types who benefit from the product but are not the primary focus.

## User Needs
Define the practical, emotional, and workflow‑based needs the product must address.

## Pain Points
List the challenges, frustrations, and gaps in existing tools that AST Studio resolves.

## Motivations
Describe what drives users to create, organize, track, or manage their studio work.

## Behaviors & Patterns
Outline how users typically work, think, and move through creative tasks.

## Environment & Context
Describe where and how users interact with the product (studio, mobile, on‑the‑go, etc.).

## Accessibility Considerations
Define the accessibility needs and inclusive design principles relevant to the audience.

## Out‑of‑Scope Users
Clarify who the product is not designed for.
