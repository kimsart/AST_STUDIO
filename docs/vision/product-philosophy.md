# Product Philosophy

## Purpose
Define the core beliefs, principles, and intentions that shape AST Studio as an artist‑first creative operating system enhanced by intelligent, assistive AI.

## Core Principles
- **Artist‑First** — The product prioritizes the lived experience, needs, and workflows of working artists.
- **Tactile & Grounded** — Interactions feel physical, sensory, and studio‑native.
- **Modular & Flexible** — The system adapts to different creative practices and project types.
- **AI as a Studio Assistant** — AI augments, supports, and accelerates creative work without overshadowing the artist.
- **Clarity & Control** — The user always understands what the system is doing and why.
