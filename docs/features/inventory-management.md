# Inventory Management

## Purpose
This document describes the system for managing measurement devices, calibration tools, and related equipment inventory.

## Recommended Sections

### Inventory Tracking
- Device registration and identification
- Location and status tracking
- Maintenance scheduling

### Calibration Management
- Calibration schedule tracking
- Certification record keeping
- Out-of-calibration alerts

### Equipment Lifecycle
- Procurement and onboarding
- Usage tracking and analytics
- Retirement and disposal procedures

### Integration Features
- Connection to measurement workflows
- Automated inventory updates
- Reporting and analytics

## Definitions
- **Inventory Management**: System for tracking equipment and devices
- **Calibration**: Process of verifying measurement accuracy
- **Equipment Lifecycle**: Complete lifespan management of devices

## Constraints
- Must comply with industry calibration standards
- Should prevent use of out-of-calibration equipment
- Cannot allow unauthorized equipment modifications
