# Social — Minors (Protected Space)

## Purpose
Provide a safe, structured, creativity-focused social environment for minors with parent approval and strict AI moderation.

## Core Principles
- Safety first
- No personal conversation
- No private messaging
- No personal data sharing
- No adult content
- No emotional bonding with AI
- Topic-only interactions

## Access Requirements
- Parent/guardian approval
- Parent dashboard visibility
- Age verification (non-invasive)
- Clear rules displayed at all times

## Room Structure

### Main Room — "Today's Workshop"
- Daily prompt
- Group participation
- AI Parent Moderator guidance

### Skill-Level Rooms
#### Beginner
- Foundational prompts
- Simple shapes, shading, color basics
- No critique

#### Intermediate
- Anatomy basics, perspective, stylization
- Technique-focused prompts
- Optional skill-based critique

#### Advanced
- Complex compositions, dynamic poses, storytelling
- Multi-step challenges
- Technique studies
- Strictly skill-based critique

### Optional Future: Masterclass Mode
- Style studies
- Composition analysis
- Multi-day challenges

## Themed Rooms (Rotating)
- Creatures & Characters
- Color & Texture
- Comics & Storytelling
- Beginner Basics
- Seasonal Themes

## Showcase Wall
- Public display of selected works
- No usernames
- No comments
- No likes
- No rankings

## Safe Upload Rules
- No faces
- No text
- No signatures
- No personal info
- No photos of the child
- No room backgrounds
- No metadata/EXIF
- Automatic filtering and blocking

## Trend Filtering
- Pulls ONLY:
  - art challenges
  - palettes
  - techniques
  - safe memes
  - seasonal themes
- Removes:
  - personal stories
  - adult humor
  - emotional content
  - political content
  - relationship content

## Parent Dashboard
- Approve/deny access
- View activity summaries
- View prompts used
- View AI moderation logs
- Adjust permissions
- No access to other kids' content

## Rules (Visible at All Times)
1. No personal info
2. No off-topic chat
3. No bullying
4. No adult content
5. Stay on the prompt
6. Ask the moderator if unsure

## Future Extensions
- Skill progression badges (non-competitive)
- Parent-approved friend groups (public only)
- Seasonal kid-safe events
