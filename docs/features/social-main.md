# Social — Main (General Users)

## Purpose
Define the adult/general social layer of AST Studio where artists can share work, discuss techniques, participate in challenges, and engage with the community.

## Core Concepts
- Open sharing environment
- Public posts and galleries
- Technique discussions
- Community challenges
- Inspiration boards
- Trend surfacing
- Optional critique (skill-based, not personal)

## User Capabilities
- Post artwork
- Tag materials, tools, and techniques
- Join challenges
- Participate in discussions
- Save references to shelves or boards
- Follow topics (not people)
- Export posts to external social media

## Safety & Moderation
- AI-assisted content screening
- No minors in this space
- No personal data sharing
- No harassment or unsafe content
- Clear community guidelines

## Interaction Patterns
- Threaded discussions
- Topic-based rooms
- Challenge-based rooms
- Inspiration boards
- Technique breakdown posts

## Trend System
- Surface trending:
  - palettes
  - techniques
  - challenges
  - styles
  - materials
- Trends feed into minors' social (filtered)

## Future Extensions
- Community events
- Collab boards
- Artist spotlights
- Seasonal showcases
